# 🎲 Black–Scholes Option Pricing Model 📈

> **From Brownian motion to Black–Scholes — a journey through randomness, probability, and the mathematics of pricing uncertainty in financial markets.**

## 📌 About

An **MSc Statistics seminar project** exploring the mathematical and statistical foundations of the **Black–Scholes option pricing model**, from Brownian motion and stochastic processes to its application on simulated and real **NIFTY 50 data**.

### 🔍 What’s Covered

* Brownian Motion & Geometric Brownian Motion
* Itô’s Lemma
* Black–Scholes PDE
* `d₁`, `d₂`, `N(d₁)` & `N(d₂)`
* European Call Option Pricing
* Volatility & Log-Return Analysis
* Simulated & NIFTY 50 Market Data
* Model assumptions and limitations

## 💡 Core Idea

> **The market may be random. The question is whether mathematics can still find structure within that randomness.**

**MSc Statistics | Stochastic Processes | Financial Engineering | Quantitative Finance**
